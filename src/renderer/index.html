    Allow full access

    macOS asks permission folder by folder. One switch, Full Disk Access, lets Gigapolis measure everything at once and you will never see another pop-up.

    1. Click Open System Settings. It lands on Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access.
    2. Turn on Gigapolis. If it is not listed, press + and choose it from Applications.
    3. Come back here. The scan starts on its own.

    Cloud and other drives

    Gigapolis only scans and cleans this computer's own disk. Cloud folders and other drives are left out, because deleting from a synced cloud drive also deletes it online and on your other devices.
